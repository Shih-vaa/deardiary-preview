What's in the app

Two halves of the same notebook.

Writing & creating

  • Rich-text editor
    Bold, italic, underline, and formatting that carries across new lines — via an editor embedded directly in each page.
  • Hand-drawn doodles
    Draw directly on any page with a real canvas layer, color picker, and undo — layered under or over your writing.
  • Floating photos
    Drag, resize, and place images anywhere on the page, the way you'd tape a photo into a physical journal.
  • A real page turn
    Pages rotate around a spine hinge and reveal the next page underneath — captured as a native snapshot mid-turn, not a slide or a fade.
  • Books, fonts & ruled lines
    Organize entries into separate books, each with its own font and optional ruled lines, plus full-text search across your whole library.

Privacy & continuity

  • Offline by default
    Everything is stored locally first. No account, no sign-up, and no connection required to write a single word.
  • PIN & biometric lock
    An app-level lock with a configurable auto-lock timer, and a PIN check before it can ever be turned off.
  • Recently Burnt
    Deleted books move to a soft-delete trash with a retention window before anything is permanently removed.
  • Encrypted cloud backup
    Optional Google sign-in backs up to a private, app-only Drive folder on a daily or weekly schedule — encrypted with an account-scoped key that's never stored on the device or in the backup itself.
  • Faithful PDF export
    Export a full book to PDF with fonts, images, doodles, and ruled-line styling exactly as they appear on screen.
Under the cover

How it's actually built.

DearDiary is a React Native app built with Expo and TypeScript throughout, with SQLite as the source of truth on-device and an optional Supabase backend for accounts and encrypted backup. It's been designed offline-first from the start — the cloud layer is an addition on top of a fully working local app, not a dependency underneath it.

→

Page turns are captured as native view-shot snapshots and rotated around a spine hinge with real perspective — not a slide standing in for one.

→

The rich-text editor runs inside a WebView; keeping its content in sync with app state across navigation, without losing a word, took real, iterative care.

→

Backup encryption keys are generated per account and gated by database row-level security — never written to the device or to the Drive folder they protect.
